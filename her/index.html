How it works

A few seconds from you.
One thoughtful action from him.

Check in when you want

Tell MVP how you are feeling. Check-ins are optional and take only seconds.

Add what matters

Save your love languages, favorite comforts, anniversary, and the details that help you feel known.

MVP guides him

He receives a daily suggestion even if you do not check in. The more you choose to add, the more tailored his guidance becomes.

Your privacy

Your body.
Your data.

Today's action, never your archive

MVP can use your current cycle and your last few days of mood information to guide his action. He cannot browse your past moods, tracking history, logs, or private notes.

Your account stays yours

Pause, unlink, switch partners, or use MVP on your own. Your account and personal history remain yours.

No ads. No data selling.

MVP has no third-party ads and never sells your personal or health information.

A note from the creator
I built MVP not only to help partners understand each other, but because, as a woman navigating hormonal changes myself, I wanted a tool that reflected our everyday reality.

I use MVP too, and it will continue to grow through the real experiences of the women who use it. If there is something that would make MVP more supportive or valuable for you, I would love to hear from you.
